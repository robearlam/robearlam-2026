Blog and video sections use `grid-cols-1 gap-8 sm:grid-cols-3` for single column on mobile and three columns at `sm+`. E2E tests confirm no horizontal overflow at 375px and 1280px viewports.

**Component/pattern alignment**: `HomepageHero` is entirely independent from `HeroSection`; the editorial component is preserved without modification. `BlogPostCard` and `VideoCard` are reused without alteration. `EngagementItem` reused for speaking entries.

**Container width**: The design note specifies `max-w-4xl` centred container. This is consistent with the design note's statement that `SiteShell` handles the outer layout boundaries, and it is consistent with how other pages on the site work. No deviation is flagged.
