In this session, Rob Earlam will go over how DevOps looks in a Composable world, he'll cover where technologies that are new to the Sitecore stack, like Vercel, fit in. Learn how to leverage these to deploy your sites at blazing fast speed. That speed doesn't come at a compromise though, we still get all the DevOps advantages we've become accustomed to like rock-solid reliability, zero downtime, and quick rollbacks when required.
