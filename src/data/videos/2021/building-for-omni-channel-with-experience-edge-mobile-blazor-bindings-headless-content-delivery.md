Sitecore Experience Edge is a set of services for headless content delivery to any touchpoint at scale, helping marketers create differentiated digital customer experiences, engage customers longer, and increase revenue opportunities.

In this video, Sitecore Technical Evangelist Rob Earlam looks at how to build a common component library in Mobile Blazor Bindings to serve content from Sitecore Experience Edge across all of your different channels.
