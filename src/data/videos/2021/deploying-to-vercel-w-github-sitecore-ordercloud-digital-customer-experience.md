Vercel is an all-in-one development platform that combines the best developer experience with a focus on delivering unmatched digital customer experience.

Vercel supports any type of application, from traditional HTML/CSS/JS sites to server-side rendered React or Next.js applications, as well as GraphQL-powered backends.

In this video, Sitecore Senior Developer Advocate Rob Earlam looks at the Vercel platform, and shows how easy it is to get started. Rob also dives into how to automate your deployments to the platform using GitHub Actions.
