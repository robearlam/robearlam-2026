This is a re-recording the session I delivered at DDD Melbourne 2019. It is an introduction to Docker & Containers. This will be an introductory session for people who have heard of Docker, but haven't had a chance to use it themselves. We cover the key concepts of Images, Containers, Volumes & Networking, and with demo's for each of these.
