A stable routine is less about meetings and more about **clear handoffs**.

> If your team can answer what changed, why, and what is next in under 2 minutes, your async loop is healthy.

## Weekly rhythm

- Monday: planning notes
- Wednesday: progress checkpoint
- Friday: short retrospective

| Day | Ritual | Timebox |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Planning update | 20m |
| Wed | Risks + blockers | 15m |
| Fri | Wins + lessons | 20m |
