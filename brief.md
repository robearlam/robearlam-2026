# Project Brief

## What is this
A personal website for an experienced technology professional
in software engineering and architecture.

## Purpose
Position the owner as a credible thought leader in their field.
A platform for both professional presence and publishing content.

## Visual Direction
Minimal and clean. White space led, typographic focus.
Primary accent colour: dark red — firm preference.
Professional but human, not corporate.
Mobile and desktop first.

## Target Audience
Potential employers, clients, collaborators and technical peers.

## Tech Stack
Next.js 16, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
